The idea

In the absence of everything, what remains is pure and luminous.

Take away the blood, the sweat, the tears. What is left is a little green goo, and it glows.

  • 発 hatsu Emission Discharge. A departure.
  • 光 kō Light A ray. A shine.
  • 体 tai Body Substance. An object.

発光体 A luminous body. A glowing thing.

It’s visceral.

A cracked skull, goo bursting out of it. Loud on purpose.

It has no depth.

A guitar is just a guitar. A skull is just a skull. Goo is just goo. Nothing hidden.

The blood is pink.

Red blood is tragedy. Pink blood is a party. Emptiness, celebrated.

Lights out.

Photographed in the dark. The green is the vinyl’s own light.

Handmade

Made by hand.

Every HAKKOTAI starts as an ordinary red guitar. This one was stripped, sanded, sprayed and wired by hand.

  2. 02Strip and catalog the electronics

  5. 05Sand the primer

  8. 08Clear coat, rewire, play

Known imperfections. A few stickers had weak glue and fought back. Others were too thick for the cutting machine and tore. We left the scars in.
