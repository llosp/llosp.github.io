README.md

I built a guitar in real life and wanted a way to show it off, so I invented a brand around it instead of just posting photos. YURA is a mock guitar company built for the HAKKOTAI, the model I built.

I designed the logo and identity, then built a full storefront site around it: a product page, a checkout flow with a working cart, team and about pages, and the whole set of legal pages a real store would ship with.
