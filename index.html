about

i'm pedro lopes, most people call me lope. i'm a designer and developer based in rio de janeiro, and i like working in the space between the two, especially when i can move past designing something and actually build it.

background

i study design at puc-rio. my background is in game development, where i worked with systems, interaction, prototyping and gameplay, and that carried into digital products, interfaces and web experiences. a lot of that work happens through prisma game lab, where i also help lead and organize projects with multidisciplinary teams.

focus

my work moves between digital product design, web, ux/ui, ai, interactive experiences and game development. i'm most interested in projects where design and technology overlap, where i can prototype an idea, understand how the system works and help turn it into something that actually runs.

toolbox

figma, unity, godot, and web technologies. ai is part of the everyday workflow, both as a build tool and as something i design with.
