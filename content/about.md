## about.md

i'm Pedro Lopes, but most people call me Lope. i'm a designer based in Rio de Janeiro
who likes to understand how things work and, when needed, code my way into making them real.

## background.md

i study design at puc-rio. my background is in game development, where i worked
with systems, interaction, prototyping, gameplay and also led projects with multidisciplinary teams,
and that carried into digital products, interfaces and web experiences.
i am currently working at ecoa institute in partnership with petrobras designing and developing
digital products to solve real company problems.

## focus.md

my work moves between digital product design, web, ux/ui, ai, interactive experiences
and game development. i'm especially drawn to challenging projects that force me to learn something new.
the kind of problems where the obvious solution isn't enough.
