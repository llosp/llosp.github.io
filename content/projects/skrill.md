## README.md

Skrill is a social productivity system for a small friend group, part habit tracker and part guild dashboard. Every season each person declares goals at a difficulty that sets the base points, and progress stays hidden until the weekly meeting, where deliveries get revealed and the group splits a shared bonus pool between each other's work.

I designed the points economy, built the whole stack solo and made a silly mascot.
