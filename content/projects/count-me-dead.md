## README.md

Count Me Dead is a boss-rush western duel about reading a countdown. You are an accountant collecting debts from a town full of outlaws, one bullet per debt, and the whole game is one button: wait for the count to hit zero, then draw.

Made in 72 hours for GMTK Jam 2026 with a team of three. I did the game design, programming and audio. Frovio and Zumate made the art.

It placed 21st out of 10,587 entries.

## problem.md

Nobody outdraws you. That is the premise, and it means the duel cannot be a reaction test, because the player would win every time.

So every outlaw cheats the count instead, each in a way that fits who they are. The design problem for the jam was making a single button carry an entire boss rush: the player only ever does one thing, so all of the variety has to come from what the count does to them.
