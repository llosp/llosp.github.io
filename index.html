03

count me dead

game design, UI, programming & audio · shipped
[-]

a boss-rush western duel about reading a countdown. you're an accountant collecting debts from a town full of outlaws, one bullet per debt, and the whole game is one button: wait for the count to hit zero, then draw. nobody outdraws you, so every outlaw cheats the count instead, each in a way that fits who they are. made in 72 hours for gmtk jam 2026 with a team of three, where i did the game design, programming and audio while frovio and zumate made the art. it placed 21st out of 10,587 entries.

~/about
about.md

i'm Pedro Lopes, but most people call me Lope. i'm a designer based in Rio de Janeiro who likes to understand how things work and, when needed, code my way into making them real.

background.md

i study design at puc-rio. my background is in game development, where i worked with systems, interaction, prototyping, gameplay and also led projects with multidisciplinary teams, and that carried into digital products, interfaces and web experiences. i am currently working at ecoa institute in partnership with petrobras designing and developing digital products to solve real company problems.

focus.md

my work moves between digital product design, web, ux/ui, ai, interactive experiences and game development. i'm especially drawn to challenging projects that force me to learn something new. the kind of problems where the obvious solution isn't enough.
