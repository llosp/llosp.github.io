The name

Three characters.
One idea.

  1. 発 hatsu Emission. To discharge. To set out.
  2. 光 kō Light. A ray. A shine.
  3. 体 tai Body. A substance. An object.

発光体 A luminous body.

Design

Visceral by design.

A cracked skull. Goo bursting out. Pink where the blood should be red.

Pink, not red.

Change the colour of the blood and the tragedy turns into a party.

24 jumbo frets.

Room to run, all the way up the neck.

No hidden meaning

A guitar is just a guitar.

A skull is just a skull. Goo is just goo. On purpose.

Handmade

Made by hand.
Every single step.

It started as an ordinary red guitar. It was stripped, sanded, sprayed, wrapped and wired back together by hand.

  1. 01Start with a red one.
  2. 02Strip the electronics.
  3. 03Sand it bare.
  4. 04Prime it.
  5. 05Sand it again.
  6. 06Layer on the pink.
  7. 07Wrap it in vinyl.
  8. 08Clear coat. Rewire. Play.

Some stickers had weak glue. Others were too thick and the cutter tore them. The scars stayed in. It is handmade, not a catalogue photo.
